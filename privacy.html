Who we are

Innovsteck is run by Mr. Sanket Rajendra Gadhave (Founder & Owner) and Miss Tanuja Sanjay Aher (Co-Founder), Maharashtra, India. Contact: innovstekprojects@gmail.com, +91 7757821670.

What information we collect

  • Name and email address that you enter when you register, and a password that you choose. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so even we cannot read it.
  • Booking and enquiry details that you type in the app: project name, course, college name and your message.
  • Login and logout times, so we know who is using the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not collect payment details in the app.

How we use it

  • To create your account and verify your email address with a one-time code (OTP) that we send to your email.
  • To reply to your project enquiries and booking requests, mainly on WhatsApp.
  • To improve our projects and services.

Where it is stored

Your name and email are saved on your own device so you stay signed in. A copy is saved in a private Google Sheet that only the owners of Innovsteck can open. When you tap "Send Request on WhatsApp", your message is sent through WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with service providers needed to run the app (Google, WhatsApp) or if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time, which removes your sign-in from your device.
  • You can reset your password at any time using "Forgot password" in the app.
  • To see or delete the information we hold about you, email innovstekprojects@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. We will act on your request within a reasonable time.

Children

The app is meant for college and diploma students and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
